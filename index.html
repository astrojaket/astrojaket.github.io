Astrophysicist · University of Oxford

Exploring the atmospheres of distant worlds.

I’m Dr Jake Taylor, a Glasstone Fellow in Astrophysics at the University of Oxford and a Supernumerary Fellow at Exeter College. I use observations, atmospheric retrievals and new computational tools to understand what exoplanets are made of.

Research

From hot Jupiters to potentially rocky worlds.

I study how light carries information about the temperature, chemistry and clouds of planets beyond our Solar System.

01 · GIANT PLANETS

Extreme atmospheres

Using transmission and emission spectroscopy to reveal the chemistry, thermal structure and clouds of hot gaseous planets.

Discover the science
02 · ROCKY PLANETS

Smaller worlds

Working to separate planetary signals from their host stars and test whether nearby terrestrial planets retain atmospheres.

Discover the science
03 · NEW METHODS

Retrievals and AI

Developing computational methods that make atmospheric inference faster, more robust and easier to compare.
